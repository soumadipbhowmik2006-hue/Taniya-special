Dear Moti,

Pata nahi ye letter kahan se shuru karoon. Jo baat dil mein hai, use bolna mere liye aasaan nahi tha, isliye socha ki likh hi deta hoon.

Hum dost hain, aur main is dosti ki bahut izzat karta hoon. Par dheere-dheere tu mere din ka woh hissa ban gayi hai jiska main bina bole intezaar karta hoon. Phone uthata hoon toh ek chhoti si umeed rehti hai ki tera message aaya hoga.

Tujhse baat karna mujhe sach mein achha lagta hai. Time kab nikal jaata hai, pata hi nahi chalta.

Shuru mein maine ise bas ek achhi dosti samjha tha. Par ab lagta hai ki meri feelings dosti se thodi aage nikal chuki hain. Ye sab itne aaram se hua ki mujhe khud nahi pata kab.

Sach bolun toh thoda dar bhi lag raha hai, kyunki tu meri bahut achhi friend hai aur main ye dosti kho nahi sakta. Isliye ek baat saaf kar doon: tujh par koi pressure nahi hai. Tera jawab jo bhi ho, meri dosti tere liye waisi hi rahegi jaisi aaj hai.

Mujhe tujhse kuch maangna nahi tha. Bas chahta tha ki tujhe meri sachchai pata ho.

Aur agar kabhi tujhe bhi lage ki hum dosti se thoda aage badh sakte hain, toh main yahin hoon. Shayad kisi din hum dono ke beech dosti ke saath-saath kuch aur bhi ho. Bas yahi ek chhoti si umeed hai. 🤍

Always yours,
Vikas ❤️
